### T-B1: Something failed on the R43-M0 midplane of the BGL supercomputer around 2005-08-19 13:56. What was it, and what happened just before and after?

**What failed:** a kernel panic. Two FATAL `KERNEL` "kernel panic" lines (pattern 576, owned by the node-platform team) were logged on R43-M0 within about 0.35 seconds of each other, starting at 13:56:00:

- 13:56:00.281 on `R43-M0-NC-I:J18-U01`, the node card's I/O node (line 3069219).
- 13:56:00.628 on `R43-M0-N8-I:J18-U11`, an I/O node on node card N8 (line 3069220).

**Just before:** I found nothing. The whole BGL dataset has no log lines in the 10 minutes before the first panic, so there was no warning, error or precursor message. The panic is the first problem-level message in that window.

**Just after:** I also found nothing. The only other lines in the following 10 minutes are the second panic. There was no recovery, restart or follow-on error logged on R43-M0 or anywhere else in the dataset. The panic burst is only those two lines on two nodes.

The logs don't show a cause. A node-platform engineer would need to look at the console or hardware side of those two I/O nodes. Both are J18 I/O nodes, which is a pattern worth checking.

The earlier two bursts of the same pattern (this was burst 3) might show whether the panic recurs on these nodes. I haven't checked that.

### T-B6: On BGL, what usually shows up on a node shortly before a 'kernel panic'?

On BGL, nothing reliably shows up before a "kernel panic" (pattern 576). It's rare: 18 lines on 18 nodes across 13 bursts, from August to November 2005. I checked the 10 minutes before each of the 18 occurrences on the same node.

- **L<N> correctable errors exceeds threshold (iar/lr)** (pattern 178, INFO, node-platform) came before 4 of the 18 panics, about 22%. It usually landed within a second of the panic (median lead about 0.4 s). The same look-back one day earlier on those nodes showed it 0% of the time. That makes it the only real precursor, though a weak one.
- **ciod: Error loading … invalid or missing program image, Permission denied** (pattern 812, FATAL, hpc-workloads) came before just 1 panic, about 21 s ahead. One case is anecdotal, not a pattern.
- **No precursor at all:** the other 13 panics, about 72%, had no other message in the 10 minutes before them on the node. In most cases the panic appears with no warning in the logs.

Treat the correctable-error threshold message as an occasional early signal, not a dependable predictor. A sample of 18 is small.

Related messages worth knowing about are "rts panic! - stopping execution" (pattern 69) and "rts: kernel terminated for reason N" (pattern 562). I didn't test what precedes them.

### T-Z3: In ZooKeeper, what else tends to happen at the same time as "caught end of stream exception"?

In ZooKeeper, "caught end of stream exception" (pattern 200062, NIOServerCnxn) has 18 bursts. Each one comes with a client session teardown and, often, a client reconnect. The co-occurrence figures below use a ±300 s window. "Lift" compares each rate with the same windows shifted by one day.

**In every burst (18 of 18, lift about 18)**
- "Closed socket connection for client /<IP> which had sessionid <HEX>". This is on the same node every time, so it is the direct consequence of the end-of-stream.
- "Processed session termination for sessionid <HEX>".
- "Expiring session <HEX>, timeout of <N>ms exceeded".

**In about 72% of bursts (13 of 18, lift about 13)**
These form a client reconnect sequence, so clients appear to drop and then come back:
- "Accepted socket connection from /<IP>"
- "Connection request from old client /<IP>; will be dropped if server is in r-o mode"
- "Client attempting to establish new session at /<IP>"
- "Established session <HEX> with negotiated timeout <N> for client /<IP>"

**Less often**
- "Got zxid <HEX> expected <N>x1" (coordination-platform): 5 of 18 bursts.
- "Creating new log file: log.<HEX>" (storage-persistence): 4 of 18.
- "Unexpected Exception:" (NIOServerCnxn): 2 of 18.
- "Exception causing close of session … ZooKeeperServer not running": 1 of 18.
- "Closed socket connection … (no session established)": 1 of 18.

**Related patterns that don't clearly co-occur**
These came up in the pattern search, but the co-occurrence results above don't link them to this one:
- "Exception causing close of session … Connection reset by peer" (200068) occurred only once, on 2015-07-29.
- "Exception when following the leader" (200069).
- "Unexpected exception causing shutdown while sock still open" (200048).

**Interpretation**
This looks like routine client-connection churn. Clients disconnect without a clean close, and the server closes the socket and expires the session. Many of them then reconnect. The pattern's problem score is only moderate (p_problem 0.55) and its cause is classed as "network or I/O". The leader/follower errors show up only rarely alongside it. Even so, the zxid and log-rolling lines in a few bursts are worth a look if one of those bursts coincided with a leader election.

### T-T2: Why are orders failing in the demo shop, and which service is the source?

Orders are failing mainly because the **payment** service can't charge cards. The frontend only reports the failure.

**Main cause: payment service (payments-team)**
- The payment service logs `Visa cache full: cannot add new item.` as an ERROR. That message appears about 11,000 times in the sampled slice, across 24 nodes.
- The checkout service passes this up as `failed to charge card: could not charge the card: rpc error: code = Unknown desc = Visa cache full...`.
- The frontend then logs `Error: 13 INTERNAL: failed to charge card...` followed by `Failed to place order`. "Failed to place order" has about 21,800 lines, and the "Visa cache full" variants of the frontend error add many thousands more.
- The failure starts at 00:00:00 on 2025-09-23, the first moment in the data. The data covers only that 30-minute window, so I can't say what happened before it.

**Secondary problems, with smaller volumes**
- **Currency service:** `failed to prepare order: failed to convert price of "<id>" to USD` (about 6,200 lines). Another error reads `failed to convert shipping cost to currency ... connection reset by peer` (about 4,200 lines). Both originate in currency or the calls to it, and both also break order placement.
- **Email service:** `NoMethodError - undefined method 'each' for nil` (about 2,100 lines). Checkout logs this as `failed to send order confirmation ... email service: expected 200, got 500`, but only as a warning. It affects confirmation emails and doesn't look like what blocks the order.

**Caveats**
- The sample is a 0.1 s slice of each minute, so the line counts are not true totals.
- I didn't trace individual requests end to end. The link between the frontend and payment errors comes from matching error text.
- I didn't find why the cache is full.

**Next steps:** Have the payments team look at the Visa cache in the payment service, for example its size limit, eviction, and any leak. Check the currency service's reliability separately.
